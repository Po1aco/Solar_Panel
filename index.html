Grounding of the figures. Santa Marta sits at roughly 5.5 kWh/m²·day, the highest band in Colombia (IDEAM/UPME solar atlas). The reference air conditioner is a Midea Solstice EZ-18RD6 — 18,000 BTU/h, 5.3 kW cooling, R32 full-DC inverter, cooling input 634–2,120 W, SEER 8.5. Market rates: 550–620 Wp monocrystalline modules at $400k–$750k COP each; residential systems at $3.5–5.5M COP per kWp; a 5 kWp array with 5–10 kWh of LiFePO₄ quoted at $52–88M COP. Physical installation runs 1–3 days, with operador de red paperwork taking 3–6 weeks. Ley 1715 of 2014 and Ley 2099 of 2021 give an IVA exclusion plus a 50% income-tax deduction, together worth up to ~40% of project cost.
